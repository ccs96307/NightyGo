# Go-stone placement recording

The five bundled WAV choices are short clips from **GoStoneSounds.ogg** by
billhails2 on Freesound:

<https://freesound.org/people/billhails2/sounds/588317/>

The field recording contains five Yunzi Go stones being placed on two board
types: three hits on a tabletop board with wooden legs and two on a thin
19×19 shin-kaya board. Freesound marks the recording CC0 1.0. The clips in
this directory were cut from its high-quality Ogg preview and converted to
mono 44.1 kHz PCM WAV for low-latency playback.

The four `capture_*.wav` pickup patterns are derived from those CC0 stone
impacts. They combine one, two, three, or five quick clacks so single stones,
small groups, groups, and large groups have distinct audible feedback.

# Cat meow placement recording

`cat_meow.mp3` is the short preview of **Cat Meow** by qubodup on Freesound:

<https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/813119/>

Freesound marks the source CC0 1.0. The 0.455-second clip is used as an
optional placement sound and is bundled as an MP3 preview.


# Additional cat voices

- `cat_meow_2.mp3` is a short preview of **Cat meow 2** by MBPL: <https://freesound.org/people/MBPL/sounds/668816/>.
- `cat_meow_3.mp3` is a trimmed preview of **cat meow short** by skymary: <https://freesound.org/people/skymary/sounds/412017/>.
- `cat_capture_yowl.mp3` is a short preview of **Angry Cat Meow** by bevibeldesign: <https://freesound.org/people/bevibeldesign/sounds/350411/>.

All three Freesound recordings are marked CC0 1.0. The yowl is played for captured stones when a cat placement sound is selected; larger captures repeat the yowl in short groups.
